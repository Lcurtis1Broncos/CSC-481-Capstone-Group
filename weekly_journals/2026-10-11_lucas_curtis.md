## What I learned

An Alternate Data Stream holds additional data attached to a host file,
separate from its normal contents. The stream name identifies that additional
data, and the byte length and SHA-256 hash provide values to check against
later recovery results.

I strengthened my understanding of ADS by writing out the steps I used in
the lab. This also helped me better understand the work needed to complete
my assigned tasks and document the results for the project.
